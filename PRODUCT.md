# Sampai

## Product purpose
Tells APU students, before they are late, that their train or bus has stalled, what to do instead, and gives them a verifiable delay letter for their lecturer. Phones are the sensors for rail (there is no public live train feed).

## Users
- **Primary:** APU students commuting on the LRT Sri Petaling line to Bukit Jalil, then the APU shuttle or a walk. On a crowded platform or train, one hand, bright daylight, anxious, glancing for 3 seconds.
- **Secondary:** any Klang Valley student on Rapid KL rail or bus.
- **Operators:** student services staff reading disruption patterns on a laptop (`/admin`), and the demo team driving `/sim` on stage.
- **Lecturers:** open the public incident page from a QR code to verify a letter.

## Tone
Calm, plain, decisive. Say what happened, what to do, and whether you will make class. No hype, no emoji in copy, no jargon. English and Bahasa Melayu.

## Strategic principles
1. The next action beats information. "Leave by 08:05" or "Take T580" first; maps and detail after.
2. Trust through evidence: rider count, confidence, data source, and SIMULATED labels are always visible.
3. Works in sunlight and on a projector: high contrast light theme.
4. One-thumb reach for the primary action on phones.

## Anti-references
- Dark neon "transit app" look (black map + mint green).
- SaaS hero-metric dashboards with gradient KPI cards.
- Glass/blur overlays, emoji-as-icons.
